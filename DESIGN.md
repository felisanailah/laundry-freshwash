## Brand & Style
The design system positions on-demand laundry and fabric care as an effortless, crystalline utility. The visual personality communicates clinical cleanliness, domestic warmth, and punctual operational reliability. 

Balancing modern SaaS rigor with high-convenience consumer e-commerce, the system utilizes an elevated minimalist style: expansive white space, light-diffusing translucent surfaces, and tactile micro-interactions that mirror the sensory qualities of freshly pressed linen and crisp water. Interfaces must never feel cluttered, utilitarian, or industrial; every view should project freshness, order, and luminous calm.

## Colors
The palette balances active watery tones against neutral slates to deliver high legibility and an immediate sensation of cleanliness.

- **Primary (`#0284C7`)**: Vibrant Cerulean drives primary actions, selected timeline checkpoints, interactive toggles, and brand markers.
- **Secondary (`#10B981`)**: Mint Emerald signals completed stages, confirmed deliveries, paid receipts, and freshness badges.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B`)**: Warm Amber marks active washing, cycle processing, and caution states without provoking panic.
- **Error / Urgent Alert (`#EF4444`)**: Retained strictly for payment declines, delicate-item confirmations, and cancellation warnings.
- **Canvas & Surfaces**: The base canvas alternates between Pure Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`) for elevated cards and elevated containers, and Soft Slate Gray (`#F8FAFC`) for structural canvases. Subsurface borders leverage translucent slate (`#E2E8F0`).

## Typography
Plus Jakarta Sans provides geometric clarity paired with friendly, humanist curves, striking the right balance between clean tech precision and domestic warmth.

- **Headlines**: Set in Bold (`700`) and Semi-Bold (`600`) with tight negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) to maintain an editorial, high-end feel.
- **Body Text**: Regular weight (`400`) at `14px` and `16px` prioritizes open vertical rhythm (`1.45x` to `1.5x` line-height) to prevent eye strain during configuration and scheduling workflows.
- **Micro-labels and Badges**: Set in Semi-Bold (`600`) or Bold (`700`) with slight positive tracking to ensure instant scanability on tracking steppers and status badges.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a fluid-responsive 12-column grid for desktop workspaces (max width `1280px`), adapting down to an 8-column layout for tablets, and a 4-column layout for mobile devices. 

Vertical spacing relies on a strict 8px increment system (`4px` for tight inline offsets). Ample outer margins ensure items never crowd screen boundaries, preserving an open and breathable spatial rhythm. When orchestrating multi-step ordering processes, the main operational canvas sits locked in a centered 720px structural column or a sticky two-column split (`65%` active selection / `35%` order summary).

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is created through subtle atmospheric layering rather than dark drop shadows. Surfaces must feel weightless, illuminated by a gentle overhead light source:

- **Level 0 (Flat Ground)**: Background canvas `#F8FAFC`, flat borders with no shadow.
- **Level 1 (Default Cards & Modules)**: Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface with a delicate border (`#E2E8F0`) and an ambient, low-opacity drop shadow: `0px 2px 8px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0px 4px 16px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Cards & Interactive Controls)**: `0px 8px 24px -4px rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.08), 0px 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, Floating Action Steppers)**: `0px 20px 40px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0px 0px 0px 1px rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`. Glassmorphic elements like sticky checkout footers and floating progress bars apply a `12px` backdrop blur over a `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` surface.

## Shapes
Geometry is friendly, soft, and modern. Standard modular cards adhere strictly to a `16px` (`rounded-lg`) corner radius, producing a softened profile reminiscent of folded towels or rounded machine portals. 

Inner controls (buttons, inputs, status pills) use unified radii: inputs and regular buttons sit at `8px` (`0.5rem`), while active status indicators, progress tags, and micro-stepper pills utilize full pill shapes (`rounded-full` / `9999px`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Vibrant Cerulean (`#0284C7`), text pure white, `10px 20px` padding, 8px border-radius. Active state subtly darkens to `#0369A1` with an immediate scale transform (`0.98`).
- **Secondary / Ghost**: White background with `#E2E8F0` border and `#0F172A` text. On hover, shifts to `#F8FAFC` background with `#0284C7` border and text.

### Cards
- Constructed with `#FFFFFF` background, `16px` border-radius, and a single `#E2E8F0` hairline stroke. Internal card padding ranges consistently between `20px` and `24px`.

### Badges & Pill Tags
- Fully rounded pills (`border-radius: 9999px`) utilizing subtle pastel background tints with deep foreground text for contrast:
  - **Success / Completed**: Background `#ECFDF5`, Text `#047857`.
  - **Processing / Active**: Background `#FEF3C7`, Text `#B45309`.
  - **Schedule / Info**: Background `#E0F2FE`, Text `#0369A1`.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Standard height of `44px` with a crisp `#E2E8F0` outline and `8px` border-radius. Focused inputs transition to a `#0284C7` border accompanied by an atmospheric ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.15)`.

### Stepper Workflow (Order & Wash Lifecycle)
- Horizontal connector lines in `#E2E8F0` with completed spans filling in `#0284C7` or `#10B981`.
- Step nodes feature `36px` circular containers: past steps display an emerald check icon, current active steps display a pulsating ring in vibrant cerulean, and upcoming steps display neutral slate indices.
